# Deity artwork — drop-in guide

Each deity is a **stack of full-size transparent layers** that are animated individually
(float, ear flap, trunk sway, blinking, glancing eyes…). The placeholders here are SVGs
generated by `tools/placeholder-art/`. Replace any of them with your own art and **no code
changes are needed**.

## The three rules

1. **Same artboard for every layer of a deity.** Ganesh Ji is `600 × 720` (5:6). Export each
   layer at the same pixel size or larger with the same aspect ratio, e.g. `1200 × 1440`.
   Never crop a layer to its content. Layers line up because they are all the same canvas.
2. **Same file name as the placeholder.** Put `body.webp` next to `body.svg` and it wins
   automatically. Priority: `.avif` > `.webp` > `.png` > `.svg`. Delete the SVG if you like.
3. **Transparent background** (PNG/WebP with alpha). Max 2048 px on the long edge, and
   ideally ≤ 1440 px. Compress with Squoosh at WebP quality 80–85.

## Ganesh Ji — `ganesh/` (600×720)

Back to front. "left/right" mean the **viewer's** left/right.

| Layer            | What goes on it                                                                                            | Animation (pivot as % of artboard)      |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `halo.png`       | Mandala/halo disc behind the head                                                                          | Slow 360° turn around (50%, 34.7%)      |
| `body.png`       | Shoulders, chest, garlands, shawl                                                                          | —                                       |
| `ear-left.png`   | Viewer's-left ear only                                                                                     | Flap: scaleX + rotate at (35.2%, 44.4%) |
| `ear-right.png`  | Viewer's-right ear only                                                                                    | Flap at (64.8%, 44.4%)                  |
| `head.png`       | Head and face **without eyes**, tusks, tilak                                                               | —                                       |
| `eye-whites.png` | Just the two eye-white shapes (also the eye mask)                                                          | —                                       |
| `irises.png`     | Irises/pupils, centred in the eye whites                                                                   | (Optional glance; off for Ganesh)       |
| `eyelids.png`    | Upper lids in the **open** position, sitting just above the eye, with the lash line along their lower edge | Slides down 3.6% of height to blink     |
| `eye-lines.png`  | Lower lash line, kajal wing, lid crease (static)                                                           | —                                       |
| `trunk.png`      | Trunk only; its top should blend into the face                                                             | Sway ±2.2° at (50%, 42%)                |
| `crown.png`      | Mukut and forehead band                                                                                    | —                                       |
| `lotus.png`      | Lotus seat in front of everything                                                                          | —                                       |

Pivots and blink settings live in `src/scenes/ganesh/ganesh.rig.ts`. If your ear
attaches at a different spot, edit the pivot there. The whole figure also floats gently.

### How the eyes work

`irises` and `eyelids` are clipped by the **shape of `eye-whites`** (CSS mask). So:

- paint the irises slightly larger than needed, because the mask trims them;
- paint each lid as a skin-coloured block **above** the eye whose bottom edge is the upper
  lash line. To blink, the lid slides down by `blinkTravel` (a fraction of artboard height),
  so the block must be at least that tall.

### Exporting from Photoshop / Procreate / Figma

Keep one document at the artboard size and one group per layer above. Export every group
as its own full-canvas PNG ("Export layers to files" with "trim" **off**), then convert to
WebP.

## Shiv & Parvati — `shivparvati/` (sprites, not full-artboard stacks)

The phere scene moves these as whole figures around the agni, so they are **cut-out
sprites** with tight bounds:

| File          | Size / facing                   | Notes                                           |
| ------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `shiv.png`    | 120×220 ratio, **facing right** | Full figure, feet at the bottom edge            |
| `parvati.png` | 120×220 ratio, **facing right** | Full figure, feet at the bottom edge            |
| `nandi.png`   | 220×150 ratio, facing left      | Seated bull beside the mandap                   |
| `garland.png` | Square                          | The varmala ring that is exchanged at the start |

They are mirrored automatically when walking left, and visibly "turn" at the sides of
the circle. Higher resolution (e.g. 480×880) is welcome, as long as the ratio is kept.
